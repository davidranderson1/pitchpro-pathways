Gating policy — enforced in code, not by convention

SurfaceFree tier showsPlus addsNever gated
Player — Who Viewed MeEvery viewer, named, with times and reasonsTrends, history beyond 30 days, exportViewer identity
Parent — Who's Been LookingEvery viewer of every child, named, with times and reasonsTrends, weekly digest, alerts, history, exportViewer identity
Guide — Who Viewed YouCounts and dates only; names blurredNames of identifiable viewers, pages watched, repeat visitorsn/a — commercial
The rule, written down because it will be argued with: identity on the child side is never a paid feature. A parent must never have to pay to learn which adult looked at their child, and a child must never be shown a blurred name with an upgrade prompt. Conversion pressure belongs on the guide tier, where the viewer is an adult in a commercial relationship. A paid tier also never overrides a viewer's own privacy setting — a subscribing guide still cannot see the name of a parent browsing privately.
