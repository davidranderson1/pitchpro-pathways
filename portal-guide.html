Player Moments

What your players choose to show you between sessions.
You are a guest here. A moment reaches you only when three things are true: you have completed a real session with that player, their parent has approved you, and the player has chosen to include you on that particular post. You cannot request access, search for players, or follow anyone. Players can remove you at any time and are never told to explain why.
How to comment. Write about the football, not the child. Coaching, encouragement, one thing to try next. Never arrange contact, never suggest moving off-platform, never comment on appearance. The parent reads every word you write, as you write it — that is the deal that makes this possible.

Parents browse semi-privately by default. Age group and city are shown because they help you judge whether a page is reaching the right families; a name is not, and is not yours to have until they choose to make contact. A returning private viewer is still a strong signal — three visits and no enquiry usually means something on the page is not answering a question.

What this screen will never show you. Who viewed a player. Nothing a child does on this platform appears in a guide's analytics, on any tier, ever. If a player has shared a moment with you, you see the moment — you do not see how often they looked at your page, and they do not become a number in your dashboard.

What PitchPro Plus includes for guides

  • Names and identities of the people who viewed you — the whole point of the tier
  • Which of your pages and videos they actually watched, and for how long
  • Repeat visitors, and who came back after an enquiry went quiet

Considered and not in this version, kept here so the decision is on the record: referral source and watch-through detail, search appearances and ranking against comparable guides, and enquiry-to-booking conversion tracking. Any of the three can be added later; none of them changes the privacy model.
