Safety & Permissions

Nora's guardrails. You set the boundary; inside it, Nora decides.

1 · How much freedom does Nora get?

You set this per child, not by age — Nora is on post-first. Change it whenever you like; changing it never affects moments already posted. Flagged moments are held for you in every mode.

Milo is 9 — his account is an exception you asked for. Under-10 accounts are not offered as standard. Where a parent asks for one, it is created locked to family only: a private timeline shared with you and nobody else. No guides, no comments, no promotion links, and the three buttons above are greyed out on his account. It unlocks on his 10th birthday and you choose the mode then.
