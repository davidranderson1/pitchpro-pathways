Sync with Google Calendar

Copy calendar link Open Google Calendar → From URL

1. Tap “Copy calendar link”.

2. Open the “From URL” page (second button — easiest on a computer), paste the link, press Add calendar.

3. “Family Football” appears in your calendar list, and syncs to the Google Calendar app on your phone automatically.

Prefer a one-off? Every event card also has its own “Add to Google Calendar” button.

Forward coach messages

Got a schedule from a coach — email, WhatsApp, or a photo of a noticeboard? Send it to the family schedule inbox. It is read every hour, translated, and added to this app and the synced calendar automatically; inferred dates arrive with the amber flag, and anything unclear comes back as a question instead of a guess.
